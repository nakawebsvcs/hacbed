bio: Roella Foronda, MPH, is the Deputy Director of the Hawai‘i Public Health
  Institute (HIPHI), where she leads strategic initiatives and organizational
  operations. Prior to that she worked 12 years with the Hawaii Community
  Foundation in philanthropy and community initiatives. With over two decades of
  experience in the nonprofit sector, Roella brings a deep commitment to
  community health and equity. She holds a Master of Public Health from the
  University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa and a bachelor’s degree in Health Science from
  San Francisco State University. Outside of work, she enjoys gardening, baking,
  and spending time with her family.
